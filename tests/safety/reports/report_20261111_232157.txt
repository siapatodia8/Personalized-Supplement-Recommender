  FINAL ANSWER:
    **You're not taking any supplements right now, so there are no supplement interactions to worry about. However, Metformin does put you at risk for Vitamin B12 deficiency.**
    
    Since you're on **Metformin**, you need to know that it depletes **Vitamin B12** in your body over time. This happens because Metformin interferes with how your intestines absorb B12 from food. Many people on Metformin for months or years end up with low B12 levels without realizing it.
    
    Low B12 can cause fatigue, nerve problems like tingling or numbness in your hands and feet, and memory issues. These symptoms can creep up slowly, so it's worth monitoring. Your doctor can check your B12 levels with a simple blood test and recommend supplementation if needed.
